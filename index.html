Storylight Cinema

Original animated stories on YouTube

Storylight Cinema publishes original fiction in three series:

  • Awakening Chronicles: anime adventures about underdogs and "useless" skills;
  • After Dark: animated scary stories, always fiction, never presented as true;
  • Frontier Hearts: Old West stories of hard land and good people.

Watch on YouTube: Storylight Cinema.

How the videos are made

Every story is written for the channel. Narration and artwork are made with AI tools, and every video says so in its description.

Storylight Cinema Studio is the channel's private publishing tool. It uploads the channel's own videos to its own YouTube channel. See the privacy policy and terms.
